# Code of Conduct

MotionBench includes students, many of them minors, alongside coaches, researchers
and industry engineers. We adopt the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
and add three project rules:

1. **Protect young participants.** No private messages to minors from adults
   outside their team's mentors; no sharing of identifiable images of minors
   without guardian consent; no personal data in issues or traces.
2. **Critique runs, not people.** Results discussions name agents and methods,
   never mock a team or a driver.
3. **No hype at others' expense.** Do not use leaderboard results to claim that a
   group of people is "beaten" or "replaced"; report what the data shows.

Report concerns to the steering group at conduct@motionbench.org. Reports are
handled confidentially and acknowledged within 72 hours.
